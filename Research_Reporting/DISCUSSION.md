# Research Discussion

**Status:** Living interpretation document. Update only when supported by derivations or executed results.

## 1. Purpose

This document interprets the mathematical and computational evidence generated by the project. It is not a substitute for the derivations in the mathematical specification or the raw evidence in `RESULTS_LOG.md`.

## 2. Current methodological position

The project has selected a single cart-mounted rigid inverted pendulum, an LQI baseline, a two-output RBF residual approximation, instantaneous input-direction projection, online RBF-weight adaptation, bounded actuation and a UUB stability objective.

These are design decisions, not yet validated performance findings.

## 3. Discussion themes to develop as evidence becomes available

### 3.1 Physical model adequacy
Discuss whether the derived model captures the relevant cart-pendulum coupling, friction, parameter changes and actuator effects. Distinguish omitted physics from uncertain parameter values.

### 3.2 Nominal LQI behaviour
Discuss equilibrium regulation, transient response, control effort and the implications of integral action. Compare only under identical initial conditions and constraints.

### 3.3 RBF approximation quality
Discuss training/approximation-domain coverage, residual errors, basis placement, scaling, extrapolation and sensitivity to measurement noise. Do not equate low training error with closed-loop stability.

### 3.4 Matched and unmatched uncertainty
Explain which residual components can be compensated through the actuator direction and which cannot. Discuss the consequences of unmatched pivot torque and modelling error.

### 3.5 Adaptation and stability
Interpret weight trajectories, projection activity, approximation-error bounds and the verified Lyapunov result. State all assumptions and operating-region restrictions.

### 3.6 Actuator constraints
Discuss saturation, slew-rate limiting, integrator windup, actuator lag and their influence on tracking and stability.

### 3.7 Robustness
Discuss parameter sweeps and disturbance cases using predeclared metrics. Identify worst cases and explain mechanisms rather than relying on selected favourable plots.

## 4. Limitations and threats to validity

Track limitations such as uncertain friction, idealised sensors, estimated accelerations, unmodelled motor dynamics, finite simulation duration, numerical solver dependence, RBF extrapolation and untested hardware effects.

## 5. Evidence discipline

For every claim, cite a derivation, a test ID, a figure/data file or a literature source. Use wording such as:
- “The derivation establishes…” for mathematical results.
- “The simulation produced…” for executed numerical evidence.
- “The result suggests…” for cautious interpretation.
- “The present study does not establish…” for limitations.

Do not write “the controller is stable” unless the applicable theorem and assumptions have been completed and checked. Do not describe unexecuted scripts as validated.
